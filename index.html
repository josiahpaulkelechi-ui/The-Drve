The opportunity

Africa's transportation economy is bigger than ride-hailing.

Transportation is one of Africa's largest economic layers, yet much of the infrastructure supporting drivers, vehicles, payments and recurring mobility remains fragmented.

01 / DRIVER

Drivers need more than trip requests.

Mobility entrepreneurs need infrastructure that helps them manage earnings, operations, vehicles and financial activity.

02 / VEHICLE

Vehicles are economic assets.

The vehicle is not simply transportation. It is an income-producing asset that needs visibility, utilization and financial support.

03 / FINANCE

Mobility and money are connected.

Every trip can create financial activity. THE DRVE connects mobility infrastructure with the financial layer around it.

04 / CONNECTIVITY

Transportation doesn't stop when connectivity does.

African mobility infrastructure must be designed for real-world connectivity conditions, not perfect digital environments.

Resilient infrastructure

Transportation doesn't operate in a perfect digital environment.

A driver can enter a connectivity dead zone. A trip can continue while a platform is temporarily unreachable. A payment event can happen before connectivity is restored.

THE DRVE is being designed around these realities — not around the assumption of perfect connectivity.
